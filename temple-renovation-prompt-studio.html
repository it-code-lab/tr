5. Free and low-cost tool guide

      
Use this as a starting stack. Free credits and pricing change often, so confirm the current plan before you commit to a long project.

      

        

          
Final image

          
Best for Scene 6 and reverse keyframes.

          
    
            
  • Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator: easy free starting point.
    • 
            
  • Leonardo AI: useful free daily tokens and good style control.
    • 
            
  • Freepik AI: low-cost suite when you want many image/video models in one place.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Reference edits

          
Best for creating Scene 5 from Scene 6, Scene 4 from Scene 5, and so on.

          
    
            
  • Leonardo AI image-to-image or canvas tools.
    • 
            
  • Freepik AI image generation with reference images.
    • 
            
  • ComfyUI with Flux/SDXL if you want the lowest long-term cost and more control.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Image to video

          
Best for forward clips between two keyframes.

          
    
            
  • Runway: strong quality; free credits are limited and do not refresh monthly.
    • 
            
  • Pika: friendly for short tests and effects; check current credit rules.
    • 
            
  • Luma Dream Machine or Kling: good motion options; free tiers are limited and may watermark.
    • 
          

        

        

          
Edit, audio, export

          
Best after clips are generated.

          
    
            
  • CapCut or DaVinci Resolve: edit, captions, sound effects, and 9:16 export.
    • 
            
  • FFmpeg: extract last frames and join clips when you want precision.
    • 
            
  • YouTube Audio Library or original music: safer devotional background audio.
    • 
          

        

      

    


    

      
6. Editing checklist

      
Use this after the AI video clips are generated.

      

        

          
Frame continuity

          
Extract each clip's last frame and use it as the next clip's first-frame reference. Use crossfades only when a handoff still jumps.

        

        

          
Audio

          
Layer temple bells, conch, tanpura or drone, soft ambience, and devotional music with proper usage rights.

        

        

          
Text overlay

          
Use minimal respectful text: short deity mantra, restoration phrase, or place label. Keep overlays secondary to the temple.

        

      

      

        FFmpeg helper commands
        

          
Extract last frame

          
ffmpeg -sseof -0.1 -i scene01.mp4 -update 1 scene01_last_frame.png

        

        

          
Concatenate clips

          
ffmpeg -f concat -safe 0 -i clips.txt -c copy final_temple_video.mp4
